Deliberately excluded (noted here for completeness, not foundation-governance roles):

These are project-level representation roles, not foundation board/officer seats, so they are excluded per the scope rule. Names are available on the same page if a project-roles dataset is wanted.
- **Advisory Board**: sponsor-nominated industry advisors (e.g. Mark Szymanski, HeroDevs). Advisory, not governance.

Paid vs volunteer: the page states no employment/pay status for any councilor or officer. No "Staff" heading exists. Treated as volunteer governance; no confidence penalty applied because roles (Councilor/Officer) are explicit, only the pay dimension is unstated.

Bios: published per person and captured verbatim.

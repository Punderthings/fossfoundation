# Eclipse Foundation AISBL — Leadership

Scope of this record: the Eclipse Foundation AISBL Board of Directors (22 directors, from the directors page) plus the eight-person "Eclipse Foundation Leadership" tier from the staff page (Executive Director, C-level officers, VPs, and the officer roles Secretary and Treasurer). Each board director is a company-affiliated seat; the representing organisation is noted in parentheses at the end of each bio verbatim from the page (no separate org-affiliation field exists in the schema).

Officers: the Secretary (Gaël Blondelle) and Treasurer (Paul White) are recorded with `role_class: officer`. Gaël Blondelle also holds the paid role VP, Community Operations, so he carries two roles. Mike Milinkovich (Executive Director) and the C-level/VP leaders are recorded as `paid_staff`.

Paid vs volunteer: the staff-page leaders are recorded `paid_staff` at confidence 1.0 because the staff page states verbatim these people work "full-time on behalf of the Eclipse community." The 22 board directors are not stated to be paid and are almost all external company representatives, so they are recorded `board_director` and not classified paid. Ed Merks appears both as a board director and, separately, on the staff page (Release Engineer for Eclipse SimRel); he is recorded once here as a director with a note.

Deliberate scope decision — general staff: beyond the eight-person Leadership tier, the staff page lists roughly 80 further foundation employees (engineers, marketers, program/project managers, IT, events, etc.). These are genuine paid staff but not foundation leadership, so to keep this a leadership record they are NOT enumerated here (mirroring the exclusion of project-level roles in the ASF record). The full staff roster is available at https://www.eclipse.org/org/foundation/staff/ and could be captured as a separate staff dataset. Flagged for retrieval if a full-staff dataset is wanted.

Not published (hence null): per-person contact and term dates. The staff page states the email convention is `firstname.lastname@eclipse-foundation.org`, but this is a pattern, not published per-person addresses, so `contact` is left null rather than synthesising individual emails. Board directors have published bios (captured verbatim, trimmed); staff-tier leaders do not have bios on the staff page (null). No term dates are stated anywhere on either page.

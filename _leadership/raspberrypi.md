# Raspberry Pi Foundation — Leadership

Scope of this record: the governing Board of Trustees (11) plus the senior executive leadership (C-suite and Executive/Managing Directors) drawn from the staff team. The Raspberry Pi Foundation is a UK charity registered in England and Wales (no. 1129409) and a company limited by guarantee.

Governance: the Foundation is governed by a Board of Trustees elected by the Membership. The org states explicitly that "Trustees and Members are volunteers", so all trustees are captured as `board_director` (unpaid governance) at confidence 1.0, with John Lazar additionally captured as `officer` (Chair of Trustees).

Members governance layer (noted, not captured as records): the Foundation also has a large volunteer Membership (~30 individuals) that elects the trustees, holds the Board to account, and contributes to strategic direction. Notable Members include the founding members David Braben, Eben Upton, and Pete Lomas (co-creators of Raspberry Pi), plus figures such as Dame Wendy Hall, Simon Peyton Jones, Martha Lane Fox, Tim Peake, Limor Fried, and David Cleevely (former Chairman). These are a volunteer electorate rather than executive/board leadership and are not captured as individual records here.

Executive leadership: the Foundation publishes a large staff roster (~180 people) at /about/meet-the-team. Only the senior executive leadership is captured here (Chief Executive, the Chief Officers for Technology & Product, Marketing, Finance, and Learning, the Director of People and Culture who is stated to be on the Leadership Team, the US Executive Director, the Director of UK & Ireland, and the Managing Director of Technology Labs). These are classed `paid_staff` at confidence 0.9 (employed charity staff listed under the team page with job titles; paid status inferred, not stated verbatim). The full operational staff (learning managers, engineers, coordinators, etc.) is deliberately not captured, consistent with excluding non-leadership operational roles.

Not published (hence `null`): per-person contact info and term dates for all individuals. Trustee and executive bios are captured verbatim (lightly trimmed) where published.

Related group entities: the Raspberry Pi Foundation Group also includes Raspberry Pi Foundation North America, Inc. (see `raspberrypi2`), Raspberry Pi Foundation Europe (Hello World Foundation, Irish charity 20812), Raspberry Pi Educational Services Private Limited (India), and Raspberry Pi Foundation Kenya.

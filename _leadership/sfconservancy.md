# Software Freedom Conservancy — Leadership

Scope: the Board of Directors (`/about/board/`) and the paid Staff (`/about/staff/`). SFC is a 501(c)(3) non-profit charity centred on ethical technology and copyleft/GPL compliance. The separately-listed Evaluation Committee and Outside Counsel are excluded here (they are advisory/external, not governance or employed staff).

Board (6 named directors): Laura Fortunato, Mark Galassi, Bdale Garbee, Bradley M. Kühn (Director-at-large), Allison Randal (Chair of the Board), Tony Sebro. The board is self-perpetuating (it appoints its own members and the Executive Director).

# KDE e.V. — Leadership

Registered non-profit representing the KDE community in legal and financial matters. The membership elects the board of directors. Record covers the current 5-member board with per-person KDE email addresses, bios, and elected years (term_start = year elected; the page gives no fixed term_end).

Paid vs volunteer: board directors are unpaid volunteers with external day jobs. KDE e.V. does employ staff and contractors, listed separately at ev.kde.org/corporate/staffcontractors/ (not captured in this record) — a follow-up fetch would add the paid_staff entries. Board contact: kde-ev-board@kde.org.

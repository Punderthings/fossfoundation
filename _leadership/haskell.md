501(c)(3) (New York) governing haskell.org, Hackage, GHC release infrastructure, the wiki, and Google Summer of Code participation. Governed by the Haskell.org Committee: 7 members on staggered 3-year terms; the members elect a chair annually. The corporation's directors are also directors of Haskell Foundation, Inc.

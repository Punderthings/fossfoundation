# OpenInfra Foundation — Leadership

Scope of this record: the OpenInfra Foundation Governing Board (18 directors captured from the live Governing Board page) and the Foundation Staff (12 employees from the Staff page). The OpenInfra Foundation (formerly the OpenStack Foundation; listed in MANIFEST as "Openstack Foundation") became part of the Linux Foundation in 2025, so it now sits within the Linux Foundation umbrella while retaining its own governing board and staff.

Board seat classes: the board is structured by membership tier. Directors carry seat labels of Individual Director (elected by individual members), Platinum Director, and Gold Director (appointed by Platinum/Gold member companies). These labels are captured verbatim in each person's role; all are `board_director` class. The Chair (Julia Kreger) and two Vice Chairs (Allison Randal, Amy Marrich) additionally hold `officer` roles.

Paid vs volunteer: the Governing Board directors are governance volunteers (each holds a day job at the listed member company, stated in their bios); they are captured `board_director` at confidence 1.0. The 12 people on the Foundation Staff page are foundation employees; they are captured `paid_staff` at confidence 0.9 (inferred from the "Foundation Staff" heading and executive titles; the site does not print an explicit employment statement per person).

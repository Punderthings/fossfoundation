# OpenJS Foundation — Leadership

Scope: the OpenJS Foundation Board of Directors (8 members) as listed on the `/about/governance` page. The OpenJS Foundation is a member-supported 501(c)(6) hosting JavaScript projects including Node.js, Electron, jQuery, webpack, Appium, and Express.

Board composition: the Board sets overall policy through the bylaws, mission and vision. Members hold seats by class: Board Chair (Abigail Cabunoc Mayes), Platinum Director (Matt Hernandez), Gold Director (Aaron Mitchell), Silver Director (Stephen Husak), End-User Director (Adrian Estrada), and three CPC Directors (Jordan Harband, Matteo Collina, Joe Sepi) who bridge from the technical council. Each director's listed "Title" is their sponsoring member company (GitHub, Microsoft, HeroDevs, Capital One, NodeSource, Socket, Platformatic, Cloudflare respectively); the schema has no per-person company field, so those affiliations are noted here rather than in a structured field.

Scope decision — CPC excluded: the same governance page lists the Cross Project Council (CPC) and its ~35 Regular Members. The CPC is described as "the top-level technical governance organization within the OpenJS Foundation" — i.e. technical governance, distinct from the Board's overall policy governance. Following the crawl rule to exclude technical-committee roles that are not foundation-level governance (mirroring how the ASF record excludes project VPs), the CPC voting members and regular members are deliberately NOT captured here. The three CPC Directors who also sit on the Board ARE captured, in their board capacity.

Not published (hence `null`): per-person contact, bios, and term dates for all board members.

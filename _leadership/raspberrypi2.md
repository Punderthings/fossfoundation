# Raspberry Pi Foundation North America, Inc. — Leadership

No separately published leadership roster was found for this entity, so this record is intentionally empty (`people: []`) pending a better source.

What this entity is: Raspberry Pi Foundation North America, Inc. is a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit and one of the group entities of the Raspberry Pi Foundation Group, alongside the UK parent (`raspberrypi`), Raspberry Pi Foundation Europe (Hello World Foundation, Irish charity 20812), Raspberry Pi Educational Services Private Limited (India), and Raspberry Pi Foundation Kenya. This group structure is stated in the footer of raspberrypi.org.

- https://www.raspberrypi.org/about/governance — the Board of Trustees and Membership of the UK parent charity; no separate NA Inc governing board is published.

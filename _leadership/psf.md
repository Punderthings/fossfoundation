Two org-live sources, both retrieved 2026-07-24:
- Board of Directors & Officers: https://www.python.org/psf/board/ (the "2025-2026" section is the current roster)

Both pages rendered as the no-JavaScript fallback, but the leadership content is in the static HTML and was captured in full.
